## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator play-testing on my two reference phones, **I want to** open https://breaktime.doibung.com, walk a low-poly office, slap NPCs into ragdolls and smash 20+ objects in a build that any commit can deploy with one command, **so that** I have measured proof that Three.js + Rapier meets the size, fps and iOS-stability budget before any gameplay phase builds on it.

<objective>
Benchmark slice: `?bench=1` plays the heaviest scene of the phase the same way every time and ends on a results screen the operator screenshots as acceptance evidence.

Purpose: D-08 says fps is only accepted from this scripted bench on real devices; D-11 raises NPCs to 8 for the ceiling.
Output: pure timeline with unit tests, bench runner, autopilot, results screen, bench e2e.
</objective>
