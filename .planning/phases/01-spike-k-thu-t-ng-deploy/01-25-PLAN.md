## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-25-01 | Tampering | edited storage values | mitigate | Only the literal '0' turns the panel off and only '1' marks the touch hint seen; anything else falls back to the defaults (unit-tested) |
| T-01-25-02 | Denial of service | storage disabled / quota / incognito throwing | mitigate | Every read and write in try/catch; the panel defaults to on and the toggle works for the session (e2e with throwing Storage) |
| T-01-25-03 | Denial of service | hint covering the joystick half or HUD buttons | mitigate | Touch hint pointer-events none, placed in the right half; e2e checks rects against every visible [data-hud-button] in landscape and portrait |
| T-01-25-04 | Tampering | markup injection through hint text | mitigate | Constant strings via textContent only; innerHTML grep gate stays 0 |
