## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator play-testing on my two reference phones, **I want to** open https://breaktime.doibung.com, walk a low-poly office, slap NPCs into ragdolls and smash 20+ objects in a build that any commit can deploy with one command, **so that** I have measured proof that Three.js + Rapier meets the size, fps and iOS-stability budget before any gameplay phase builds on it.

<objective>
Smash slice: cups, monitors and plants shatter into tinted shards that clean themselves up, other props clatter with drop sounds, and a scenario switch smashes the whole office for tests and the benchmark.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 (≥ 20 objects broken/knocked at ≥ 30 fps) and D-13's bounded, cheap debris.
Output: pure debris budget and impact rules with tests, shard kit, shard pool, breakables system, contact-force routing, breakables e2e.
</objective>
