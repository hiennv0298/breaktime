## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator play-testing on my two reference phones, **I want to** open https://breaktime.doibung.com, walk a low-poly office, slap NPCs into ragdolls and smash 20+ objects in a build that any commit can deploy with one command, **so that** I have measured proof that Three.js + Rapier meets the size, fps and iOS-stability budget before any gameplay phase builds on it.

<objective>
Measurement slice: a debug HUD the operator can toggle on desktop and phone, plus three quality tiers chosen automatically from measured frame times and overridable by hand. Also handles WebGL context loss visibly.

Purpose: TECH-07 and D-21; the benchmark (01-17) and the real-device gate (01-19) read these numbers and tiers.
Output: pure quality and frame-stat modules with unit tests, runtime quality manager, HUD, pause-menu controls, hud e2e.
</objective>
