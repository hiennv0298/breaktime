## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator play-testing on my two reference phones, **I want to** open https://game.doibung.com, walk a low-poly office, slap NPCs into ragdolls and smash 20+ objects in a build that any commit can deploy with one command, **so that** I have measured proof that Three.js + Rapier meets the size, fps and iOS-stability budget before any gameplay phase builds on it.

<objective>
Stability slice: a 15-minute soak mode and a crash beacon make "no crash in 15 minutes on Safari iOS" measurable, a headless leak proxy guards against regressions, and the finished measurement build is deployed for the phones.

Purpose: TECH-04 and ROADMAP success criterion 4; the gate in 01-19 needs the deployed bench + soak build.
Output: pure beacon rules with tests, beacon + banner, soak loop, soak-leak e2e, live deploy.
</objective>
