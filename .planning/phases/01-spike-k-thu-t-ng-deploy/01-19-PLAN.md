## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator play-testing on my two reference phones, **I want to** open https://breaktime.doibung.com, walk a low-poly office, slap NPCs into ragdolls and smash 20+ objects in a build that any commit can deploy with one command, **so that** I have measured proof that Three.js + Rapier meets the size, fps and iOS-stability budget before any gameplay phase builds on it.

<objective>
Real-device gate: record the two reference devices, have the operator measure the deployed build on them, and write a verdict against the ROADMAP gate. This is the evidence the phase is accepted on; "chạy mượt" in words is not accepted.

Purpose: TECH-03/TECH-04 can only be proven on real hardware (D-24); D-06 fixes the devices for all later phases; D-07 decides what happens on failure.
Output: 01-DEVICE-LOG.md, screenshots in evidence/, 01-GATE.md with VERDICT.
</objective>
