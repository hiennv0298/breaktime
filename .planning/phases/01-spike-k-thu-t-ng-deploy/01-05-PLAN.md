## Phase Goal

**As a** solo operator play-testing on my two reference phones, **I want to** open https://game.doibung.com, walk a low-poly office, slap NPCs into ragdolls and smash 20+ objects in a build that any commit can deploy with one command, **so that** I have measured proof that Three.js + Rapier meets the size, fps and iOS-stability budget before any gameplay phase builds on it.

<objective>
Asset slice: the committed, optimised CC0 content every visual slice consumes, produced by a reproducible dev-time pipeline. After this plan `src/assets` holds the office props, food props, one shared character GLB, 18 textures and the SFX set, with attribution.

Purpose: TECH-02 size budget depends on meshopt + palette merge and one shared character; D-15 needs MP3 for iOS; D-25 forbids system installs.
Output: `assets-src/fetch-kenney.mjs`, `assets-src/build-assets.mjs`, `assets-src/asset-map.json`, optimised files in `src/assets`, `CREDITS.md`.
</objective>
